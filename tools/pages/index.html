PhoneBox

★ GitHub

Turn an old Android phone into a desk panel for your computer (Windows, Linux or macOS): 55 widgets, a visual layout editor, AI coding sessions and quotas, music, webhooks. This is the real panel running in your browser with made-up data — swipe it, tap it.

✎ Open the layout editor ↗

Changes you make in the layout editor show up here right away. Everything stays in your browser.

113 widgets, 30 templates

Smart Stack, clocks, weather, calendars, media, gauges, charts, processes, Docker, OBS, Home Assistant, rules, timers, checklists, markets… on a 12 × 6 grid, as many pages as you like.

AI coding at a glance

Claude and Codex quotas with a burn-rate forecast, every Claude Code session, and Allow / Deny for permission prompts right on the phone.

Webhooks

Scripts, CI and Home Assistant push values and notifications with one curl; the panel can switch pages on demand.

USB or Wi-Fi, no cloud

Over the USB cable, or over Wi-Fi / Tailscale with a pinned certificate and a key. Nothing leaves your machine.

Install: ./scripts/install.sh --apk (Linux, macOS) or .\scripts\install.ps1 -Apk (Windows) — see the quick start.
